Cable & Cord Management

The unglamorous fix that makes the biggest visible difference: getting chargers, bricks, and the power strip off the floor and out of the webcam frame.

Also featured on: Home Office on a Budget · Desk Setup Ideas

5 min readUpdated Aug 2026

You do not need a custom desk with grommets. You need a place for the power strip, a path for the cables you actually use, and something that stops the charging cord from sliding onto the floor three times a day.

What to actually look for

  • No-drill or clamp-on — most rental desks and IKEA tops hate screws
  • Fits a real power strip, not just two thin cables
  • Leaves one “daily” cable reachable (phone, laptop) instead of hiding everything
  • Adhesive that comes off a painted wall or laminate without a souvenir
